# Generator: Shared Reasoning Evolution

The methodology evolves from observed collaboration rather than design.

New abstractions should explain multiple observed behaviours while
reducing workflow friction.

## Generator: Epistemic Grounding

Accepted knowledge ultimately depends on externally grounded
observations.

Inference organizes knowledge but does not replace evidence.

## Boundary

Do not promote stylistic preferences or individual model behaviour into
the protocol without repeated supporting observations.

## Candidate Anchors

- Human or externally grounded observations are the epistemic linchpin.
- GBA reduces representation size while preserving conceptual
  reconstruction.
- SHRUG provides the collaborative feedback loop through which the
  methodology itself evolves.
